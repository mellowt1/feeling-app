## 2. Per-place fix list (biggest visual gain first)

### Key West (fullest list — the plan's worst offender)

1. **Water crest, G5.** Flatten `w1` (`index.html:1871`, `.keywest svg.w1`) from its current 26.7 px sine to a ≤3 px line — copy Hawaiʻi's `w1` shape (`index.html:1918`) and keep Key West's own `w2`/`w3`, which already match Hawaiʻi's. This alone gives Key West a horizon; SCENE-GRAMMAR calls it "the single highest-value fix in the plan."
2. **Lighthouse scale, G6.** `.keywest .lighthouse` (`index.html:1161`, currently `width:6px;height:22px`) draws taller than the key it stands on (`svg.key` path, `index.html:1872`); shrink it so it reads under the key's own silhouette height, matching the "never taller than the landform" rule.
3. **Near-tier tone, G2.** `.keywest .palm .fig` (`index.html:1043`, `opacity:.55`) and `.arm` (`index.html:1044`, `stroke-opacity:.55`) sit at mid tone; raise to full `--silhouette` opacity so the figure reads as the nearest object in the scene.
4. **Egret and frigatebird scale, G6.** The egret (34 px = 1.11× the figure) and the frigatebird (0.92 at 74 % band) need shrinking; find their `<svg>` sizing in the keywest markup block (1866–1946) and bring both under 0.65 (ground) / monotonic-with-height (flyer).
5. **One hero, G8.** `.glitter`, `.lighthouse` beam, `.setting-sun` (`index.html:1150`), `.green-flash` (`index.html:1154`) and `.crux` twinkle (`index.html:1027`) are five separate light effects; keep the lagoon glitter by day and the lighthouse beam by night as the one hero, remove or fold the sun disc, green flash and star twinkle into flat fill.
6. **Ambient-tier fixes, G9.** `kwRuffle` (36 px / 4.4 s) and `bobGust` (8 px / 3.2 s) — both flagged in `design/v4/LINT.md` — exceed the 4 px / ≥5 s ambient ceiling; slow or shrink both.
7. **Cast trim, G12.** Drop gull, iguana, mullet, barracuda, bonnethead, school and the cats (unless Paul asks for them back) to bring 15 actors down to the target 8 in the plan's cast table.
8. **Hard-coded colour, G7.** `.waves::after { background: linear-gradient(rgba(233,169,124,.18), …); }` (`index.html:1389`) is a literal value shared verbatim with the dock's; replace with a `--glare` variable (lowest visual gain of this list, but it's the exact line `lint.mjs` will keep flagging).

### Campo

1. **Horizon height, G3.** The range's foot sits at ≈50–51.4 % instead of 62 %; the markup translate on the range group (`index.html:1808` area) needs to lift roughly 11 points so the sky/land line matches the other four places.
2. **Mid-band depth, G1.** `.steppe-mid`/`.steppe-front` (`index.html:901`) currently give the mid band only 13 points of depth against Key West's 30; once the range moves per item 1, re-check this band reads as one clear tier, not two middles.
3. **Near-tier tone, G2.** `.campo .fence .fig` (`index.html:933`, `opacity:.55`) and `.arm` (`index.html:934`) are at mid tone; raise to full `--silhouette`.
4. **Ambient overload, G9.** 173 ambient targets, 135 of them coirón blades on `bladeFlutter` at 1.7/2.3/1.4 s (`index.html:924`–`:926`); collapse the per-blade animation to a shared clump sway well above the 5 s floor, or cut the blade count.
5. **Shared clock, G11.** 16 non-ambient durations with several off-multiple values (80s, 90s, 150s, 420s); move guanaco, condor, truck etc. onto 240 s and its clean multiples/fractions.
6. **Cast trim, G12.** Drop hare, piche and the poplars to bring 12 actors down to the target 8.

### Dock

1. **Movers cap, G10.** Median 17 movers (worst 21) is the largest single number in the whole audit; the eleven `fish-wrap` cycles (`index.html:1769`–`1789`) are almost all visible at once — stagger or cut so no more than 3 move in any window, matching Hawaiʻi's median of 3.
2. **Cast trim, G12.** Drop jack (`fw4`, `index.html:1771`), barracuda/cuda (`fw5`, `:1772`), mackerel (`fw8`, `:1781`), blacktip (`fw10`, `:1788`), bonnethead (`fw11`, `:1789`), mullet (`.mullet-wrap`, `:1790`–`1802`), manatee (`index.html:1741`) and dragonfly (`:1742`) to bring 17 actors down to the target 8 (tarpon, snook, mangrove snapper group, pinfish school, grouper, heron, dolphins, cat).
3. **Shared clock, G11.** 38 distinct durations across 102 animations, the worst spread in the app; once the cast is trimmed per item 2, put what's left on a 240 s base and its multiples.
4. **Ambient-tier fixes, G9.** `snapHover` (6px/14s), `glitterDrift` (8px/18s), `bobRain` (8px/4s) and `bobGust` (8px/1.4s), all flagged in `design/v4/LINT.md`, exceed the 4 px / ≥5 s ambient ceiling.
5. **Water wavelength, G5.** `w1` (`index.html:1732`) is 71 px wide against the ≥195 px floor; widen the wave period (this is a smaller visual gain than items 1–4 since the crest amplitude and scroll speed are already in range).
6. **Hard-coded colour, G7.** `.waves::after { background: linear-gradient(rgba(233,169,124,.22), …); }` (`index.html:1241`); replace with a `--glare` variable, same fix as Key West's.
7. **Scale check, G6.** Not measured in SCENE-GRAMMAR; verify the heron (`index.html:1740`) against the 0.25–0.65 resident band once the cast trim in item 2 is done and re-shot.

### Hawaiʻi (closest already; smallest list)

1. **Movers cap, G10.** Median is exactly 3, but the worst window hits 5 (koaʻe, ʻiwa, honu, two ʻakekeke together); stagger cycle offsets so the worst window also stays ≤4.
2. **Shared clock, G11.** 12 non-ambient animations on 11 different periods (900/320/300/240/200/150/120/105/90/70/60 s); this is the one rule Hawaiʻi fails outright — move residents/visitors onto a 240 s base and simple multiples/fractions of it, the way Key West already does.
3. **Near-tier tone, G2.** `.hawaii .palm .fig` (`index.html:1564`, `opacity:.6`) is the highest of the three "mid tone" places but still short of full `--silhouette`; raise to 1 for consistency once dock's own near tier is confirmed.

### Tokyo

1. **Sky variance, unscored (report only).** All four sit-mode shots (`tokyo-{morning,day,evening,night}-sit.png`) are visually identical — there is no `html[data-place="tokyo"][data-sky="…"]` rule anywhere in `index.html` (only the single block at `index.html:1580`), so the place never changes with time of day the way the other four do. Fix: add morning/day/evening/night palette overrides the way dock/campo/keywest/hawaii already have them.
2. **Horizon, G3.** `.tk-scene` (`index.html:1586`) is a fixed 390×420 px canvas; `.tk-farSkyline`'s baseline (y=234, markup `index.html:1947`) sits at a fixed 186 px above the floor instead of 62 % of `.waves` height, so it's the only place whose horizon visibly moves between Today (78.8 %) and sit mode (50.8 %). Re-pin it to the 62 % band the other four places share.
3. **Silhouette discipline, G4.** 16 `stroke-width` attributes and 6 gradients (`index.html:1947` markup) make Tokyo the one drawn-not-silhouette place; convert the skyline and bridge to filled paths where the grammar allows it, keeping the drop-shadow/blur budget on Godzilla only (G8 already passes).
4. **Ambient-tier fixes, G9.** `tkDrift` (12px/14s), `tkShimmer` (5px/6s, twice) and `tkRings` (19.1px/0.28s), all flagged in `design/v4/LINT.md`, exceed the 4 px / ≥5 s ambient ceiling.
5. **Movers census, G10.** Re-run the census grouping Godzilla + boat + reflections as one actor before scoring; the raw count (median 6) likely resolves to ≈3 once that grouping is applied, per SCENE-GRAMMAR's own note.
6. **Water, G5.** Not measured — the bay's shimmer/streak texture needs its own test before it can be scored against the sine-wave rule at all.
